# HR Renergy Homepage

Build a modern, professional homepage for "HR Renergy" — a renewable energy 

and home-utility solutions company based in India. This is a landing page 

only for now (I'll ask for inner pages later).

BRAND & TONE

- Company name: HR Renergy

- Industry: Renewable energy & home utility systems (solar, water heating, 

  heat pumps, batteries, water softening, voltage stabilizers)

- Tone: Trustworthy, technical-but-approachable, clean corporate — similar 

  spirit to suntropsolar.com and ecosoch.com, but more modern, with softer 

  rounded cards, generous white space, and subtle motion instead of a dense 

  traditional layout.

- Color palette: Deep energy-green (#0F7A3D or similar) as primary, warm 

  amber/solar-orange (#F5A623) as accent, off-white/light-gray backgrounds, 

  charcoal text. Avoid pure black/pure white — soft, warm neutrals.

- Typography: A modern geometric sans for headings (e.g. Sora, Plus Jakarta 

  Sans, or Manrope), a clean readable sans for body (Inter).

- Fully responsive: mobile-first, works cleanly at phone/tablet/desktop.

PRODUCTS TO FEATURE (6 core offerings — give each equal visual weight)

1. Solar Power Plants (rooftop/on-grid/off-grid/hybrid systems)

2. Solar Water Heaters

3. Heat Pump Solutions

4. Batteries & UPS

5. Domestic Water Softeners

6. Servo Stabilizers

PAGE SECTIONS (in order)

1. Top utility bar

   - Left: phone number with call icon, email with mail icon

   - Right: social icons (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube)

2. Sticky header

   - Logo left (placeholder text logo "HR Renergy" for now)

   - Center/right nav: Home, About, Products (dropdown with all 6 products), 

     Projects, Blog, Contact

   - Right: "Get Free Quote" pill button, primary green, hover darkens

   - Hamburger menu on mobile with slide-in panel

   - Dropdown submenus fade+slide in on hover (desktop), accordion-style on 

     mobile

3. Hero section

   - Two-column: left = headline + subheadline + two CTA buttons 

     ("Get a Free Consultation", "Explore Products"), right = large hero 

     image of solar panels on a rooftop with soft gradient blob shapes 

     behind it

   - Headline theme: reducing electricity bills / powering homes and 

     businesses sustainably

   - Small trust badge above headline (e.g. "X+ Years of Experience")

4. Trust stats strip

   - 4 stat cards in a row (icon + big number + label): Years of Experience, 

     Projects Completed, MW/kW Installed Capacity, Happy Customers

   - Card style: white background, soft shadow, rounded corners, icon in 

     colored circle

5. Products/Solutions grid

   - Section heading: "Our Solutions"

   - 6 cards in a responsive grid (3 columns desktop, 2 tablet, 1 mobile), 

     one per product listed above

   - Each card: icon or image, product name, 1-2 line description, 

     "Learn More" link with arrow icon

   - Hover effect: card lifts slightly, border/shadow changes to accent color

6. Why Choose Us

   - 3-4 value props (Quality, Certified Experts, After-Sales Support, 

     Sustainability) as icon + short text, in a row or 2x2 grid

7. How It Works / Process

   - Simple 4-step horizontal process (Site Visit & Consultation → Custom 

     Design → Installation → Commissioning & Support), connected by a line 

     or arrows, numbered circles

8. Featured Projects / Case Studies

   - Section heading: "Recent Projects"

   - 3-4 project cards with image, project name/location, one-line result 

     (e.g. "60kW rooftop solar for XYZ Industries")

   - "View All Projects" button at the end

9. Testimonials

   - 2-3 column carousel or grid of customer quotes with star ratings, 

     customer name and location/company

10. Call-to-action banner

    - Full-width colored band (primary green or gradient) with a short 

      prompt ("Ready to go solar and save on your bills?") and a button

11. Contact section

    - Two columns: left = address, phone, email, business hours as icon 

      rows; right = a contact form (Name, Phone, Email, Product Interest 

      dropdown showing all 6 products, Message, Submit button)

12. Footer

    - 4 columns: Company info + logo + social icons, Quick Links, Our 

      Products (list all 6), Contact info + business hours

    - Bottom bar: copyright line

INTERACTIONS

- Smooth scroll for anchor links

- Nav dropdowns: hover-fade on desktop, tap-to-expand on mobile

- Product and project cards: lift + shadow on hover

- Buttons: slight scale/darken on hover, visible focus states

- Sticky header that adds a shadow once the page is scrolled

Use placeholder images (solar panels, rooftop installations, heat pumps, 

batteries, water softener units, stabilizers) and placeholder body copy 

where needed — I'll swap in real content and photos later. Keep the code 

clean and componentized so I can hand it to a developer to convert into a 

WordPress/Elementor-ready static template afterward.
